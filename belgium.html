Belgium Travel Guide

Discover the most beautiful cities in Belgium! History, architecture, and unforgettable places.

1. Brussels

Brussels is the capital of Belgium and an important European city. It is famous for its beautiful architecture, historic squares, museums, and delicious Belgian chocolate.

What to see: Grand Place, the Atomium, and Manneken Pis.

Official Brussels Tourism Website

2. Bruges

Bruges is one of the most beautiful medieval cities in Belgium. It is famous for its romantic canals, cobblestone streets, old buildings, and charming atmosphere. It is often called the Venice of the North.

What to see: the Belfry of Bruges, Market Square, and the beautiful canals.

Official Bruges Tourism Website

3. Ghent

Ghent is a historic Belgian city with a wonderful combination of medieval architecture and modern culture. It is known for its beautiful riverside views, impressive castles, and lively atmosphere.

What to see: Gravensteen Castle, Saint Bavo's Cathedral, and Graslei waterfront.

Official Ghent Tourism Website

4. Antwerpen

Antwerpen is a beautiful port city in northern Belgium. It is famous for its diamond district, fashion, art, and impressive historical buildings. The city was also home to the famous painter Peter Paul Rubens.

What to see: Antwerpen Central Station, the Cathedral of Our Lady, and Grote Markt.

Official Antwerpen Tourism Website
